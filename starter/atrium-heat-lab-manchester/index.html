      6 · Assumptions and limits

      What this tool does not know

      Change a proxy assumption and every chart updates. EnergyPlus results do not change; to change those, re-run the sweep (tools/sweep_atrium.py).

      Interpolation error, measured

      EnergyPlus model

      • One well-mixed zone: no stratification, so the upper galleries will be hotter than shown.
      • Walls and floor below 19.35 m exchange no heat with the rest of the building; 1,000 m² of exposed concrete adds thermal mass.
      • Ventilation is a fixed air-change rate per hour, not wind- or stack-driven airflow.
      • The roof screen is a uniform horizontal plane with constant transmittance, not a real product or kinetic system.
      • One crude 2050 heatwave week. Other weeks, years and climate models will give different numbers.

      Proxy assumptions (editable)

      • Daylight: BRE average daylight factor for roof lights. No glare, no sun patches, no spatial distribution.
      • Winter loss: conduction through the atrium glazing only, present-day weather, screen assumed retracted. Lost winter solar gain from low g-values is ignored.
      • Vent opening: stack effect only, equal high and low openings, no wind.

      Not modelled at all

      • Cost, embodied carbon and maintenance of any intervention.
      • Occupant behaviour and clothing. Adaptive comfort (TM52) and air speed are approximated in Diagnose.
      • Electric lighting energy that a darker atrium would need.
      • Acoustics, rain, security and fire strategy for large openings.
      • Uncertainty in the weather itself.

      Where a Track 01 team could take this

      • Add an objective (carbon, cost, daylight from Radiance) and show the new Pareto front.
      • Replace the grid with active sampling where the score changes fastest.
      • Show uncertainty: run more weather years or climate models and plot score ranges.
      • Explain the system: connect inputs to heat flows (solar, ventilation, mass) for a chosen hour.
