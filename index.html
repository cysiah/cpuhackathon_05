01 · WhyThe key finding

Even the outdoor air fails

Score the street itself with the same metric and the same week. In 2050 the air outside is already over the line, so to pass, the atrium has to stay cooler than outdoors at peak hours.

3K·h outdoor air today
46.8K·h outdoor air in the 2050 file
83K·h with 2 °C more warming

Only cool night air stored in the concrete can do that. Night ventilation alone takes the baseline from 320.6 to 224.8 K·h.

03 · Our toolFloorplate heat map

Where on each floor does it overheat, and does your fix work?

The official score is one number for the whole atrium. This tool puts it on the plan: the ground floor and the upper floor, spot by spot, from the real hot-week weather and the real EnergyPlus runs. Set a grade from the layers you care about, then change the glass, the roof screen or the ventilation and see what the change does to each floor and to the daylight.

Two lenses on the same data: the architect (where and why) and the developer or estates manager (how much floor area is fixed, at what cost to the light).

    03 · Our toolTrade-offs and honesty
